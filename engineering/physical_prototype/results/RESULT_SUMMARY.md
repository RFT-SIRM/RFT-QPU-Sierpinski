## Level-1 thermal scale

For `J/(2 pi)=10 MHz`, `Delta_leak/(2 pi)=13.937 MHz`. At 10 mK, `kBT/h=208.366 MHz`; the equilibrium benchmark `exp(-Delta/kBT)=0.9353`. At this temperature the value of `J/(2 pi)` required for a 1% Boltzmann factor is 688.49 MHz. This is a thermal benchmark only; a real driven circuit requires measured bath spectral density and population dynamics.

## Disorder checks

For 250 seeded level-1 samples, independent positive scalar edge weights in `1 +/- 10%` with the matching weighted-degree diagonal preserve the Kramers-like pair to numerical roundoff; the minimum sampled leakage gap was `1.31095 J`. Random local z fields deliberately break the effective antiunitary symmetry and split the pair. These are small-device numerical diagnostics, not fabricated-device tolerance claims.
